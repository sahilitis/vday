For You

Something I meant, written the only way it would come out right.

she is like the sea at dusk

She is like the sea at dusk,
blue, quiet, endless in ways
people never fully understand.

She lets songs speak for her,
lets old melodies carry
the weight she never names.

Maybe that is why
people who truly listen to music
sometimes become music themselves.

She loves being heard,
not loudly,
just softly enough
to know someone stayed.

Like rain hidden inside clouds,
she carries storms without thunder.
Fights wars no one notices.
Smiles, while entire oceans
move underneath her ribs.

And still, a little reassurance
reaches her the way moonlight reaches waves,
gentle, unnoticed by most,
but enough to calm the tide.

Her heart belongs to a few people only,
the rare kind she calls home.
Core friends, like old trees in winter,
silent, but always standing beside her.

She has the taste of midnight songs,
of Joji playing in a dim room,
where loneliness somehow sounds beautiful.

Blue is her favourite colour
because blue understands her,
the sky that says nothing,
the deep rivers that keep moving,
the flowers that bloom quietly,
the evening that never begs to be noticed.

And maybe that is who she is:
not meant to be fully known,
only deeply felt.

Written by someone who's still figuring out how to say all of this out loud.

Sahil 🌊
