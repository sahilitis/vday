My Vasvi,

 

Happy birthday to the girl who'll play a Joji song and call it "just background noise" while meaning every word of it for someone. You let songs say what you won't — I've been on enough video calls with you before sleep to know that's just how you work.

 

You're guarded with almost everyone and somehow completely yourself around the four or five people you actually trust. I still don't fully know how I ended up on that list, but I'm not asking questions I don't need answered.

 

You're blue through and through — quiet on the outside, an entire ocean underneath, and not remotely interested in explaining yourself to people who haven't earned it. But I've learned reassurance lands louder with you than you'll ever admit, so here it is anyway: you're doing great. You are loved. Not just today.

 

Also, separately — you would fight a small war over the last brownie, and I have never once doubted you'd win.

 

My favourite version of you lives in small, specific things: recommending Andhra Bhavan biryani like your reputation depended on it (it was a 10/10, obviously), that whole uncertain week of metro rides to the airport I'd rewind in a heartbeat, and the first time I held your hand and realised this had quietly stopped being casual. Every hug since has somehow topped the one before it, which shouldn't be possible, but you keep making it happen anyway.

 

We still owe ourselves that tea and biscuits we keep almost having. Still coming. And Wednesdays are already spoken for — Kalsang, Wongdhen, you.

 

So here's a whole website, because a text felt too small for someone who fights her battles quietly and still shows up smiling. You've been a 10/10 since day one. Today doesn't change the score — it just makes it official.

 

— forever your Sahil 🌻
